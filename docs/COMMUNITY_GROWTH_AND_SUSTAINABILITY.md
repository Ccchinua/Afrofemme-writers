# Afrofemme Writers — Community Growth and Sustainability

## Current position

The platform has the foundations for public stories, member profiles, follows, reading-list bookmarks, reading history, community posts, reactions, replies, reposts, message requests, notifications and moderation. At the latest database check, the project had one profile and no published stories, follows, bookmarks, reading-history rows, community posts, messages or content reports. Those counts are a cold-start snapshot, not a measure of community quality.

Do not present member-level analytics or neural recommendation performance as established while the platform has insufficient activity.

## Privacy-conscious community analytics

Build an aggregate-only dashboard after meaningful, consented activity exists. Useful measures include:

- Published stories and active writers over time.
- Distinct story readers, meaningful-read completion bands and returning readers.
- Aggregate bookmarks, appreciations, comments and community-post activity.
- New-writer exposure and distribution across authors, formats and counties where location is voluntarily provided.
- Moderation report volume and resolution time, shown only to authorized moderators.

Privacy rules:

- Never display a member's individual reading history, private messages, private profile details or message-request activity in analytics.
- Use aggregate counts and suppress small cohorts (initial proposal: fewer than five members) where an individual could be inferred.
- Keep analytics access limited to authorized platform stewards; do not grant editorial moderators broad access to private member behavior.
- Do not add invasive tracking, third-party ad pixels or raw search-query profiling.
- Document what is measured, why it is measured, retention periods and member controls before enabling collection.
- Keep operational security logs separate from product analytics.

## Writer support and sustainability — proposal only

No payment integration, pricing, billing or paid access is authorized by this roadmap. Potential models to research with writers before any decision:

1. **Sponsored literary programmes:** a partner funds a writing challenge, anthology, workshop or prize, with transparent sponsorship disclosure and editorial independence.
2. **Institutional and cultural grants:** seek support from arts organizations, libraries, education programmes and cultural foundations for community programming and accessibility.
3. **Optional member support:** explore voluntary contributions only after validating willingness to support the platform without locking existing writers out of core publishing.
4. **Workshops and events:** paid external workshops or ticketed gatherings could help fund operations, while the core community remains accessible.
5. **Anthologies and print collections:** curate opt-in collections with clear author consent, licensing, revenue-sharing terms and withdrawal rules.

Before any commercial launch, obtain member feedback, publish transparent terms, confirm Kenyan legal/tax and data-protection obligations, define ownership and revenue-sharing, and obtain explicit approval for payment-provider integration and any pricing.

## Release gates

1. Do not launch analytics until the metrics and consent/notice approach are documented.
2. Do not expose small-cohort statistics or individual reading histories.
3. Validate each sustainability idea with writers; do not assume demand.
4. Keep core writing, reading, profiles and community participation usable without payment.
5. Require explicit project-owner approval before adding payment APIs, checkout, subscriptions, fees or pricing.
