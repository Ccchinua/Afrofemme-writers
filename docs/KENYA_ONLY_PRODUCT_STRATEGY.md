# Afrofemme — Kenya-only product strategy

## Positioning
**Afrofemme. Kenya connects here.**

Afrofemme is a Kenya-focused social and creative community for people who want to connect, express themselves, discover local culture and make things together. It is open to all genders, backgrounds and creative disciplines. Its founding literary and women-centred heritage remains part of its story, but membership and participation are not restricted by gender or profession.

## Geographic scope
- Serve Kenya's 47 counties only.
- Use county and town as optional discovery context, not as barriers to participation.
- Set Kenya as the platform country. Do not offer other countries as profile locations or present the product as a pan-African network.
- Keep language preferences open to Kenyan languages and other languages used by members in Kenya.
- Prioritise local relevance: county-level creatives, gatherings, opportunities, circles and cultural discovery.

## Three connected experiences
1. **Social:** member feed, posts, comments, reactions, follows, messaging and 24-hour Moments.
2. **Culture:** Kenyan stories, poetry, film, music, fashion, photography, visual art, languages, heritage and contemporary culture.
3. **Opportunity:** creative circles, collaborations, challenges, gatherings, portfolios and a creator marketplace.

## Product principles
- Kenya-first, mobile-first and low-data conscious.
- Open to everyday social members as well as professional and emerging creatives.
- Preserve a distinctive beadwork-inspired visual identity without reducing Kenya's many cultures to one generic motif.
- Make publishing simple. Do not impose unnecessary editorial approval on ordinary member posts.
- Build safety into the product: clear reporting, blocking, moderation, privacy controls and auditable administrator permissions.
- Treat county and language as useful context, never as a ranking of people or communities.

## Roadmap
### 1. Kenya-only foundations
- Set the platform country to Kenya in member profile updates.
- Use the 47 counties as the optional location taxonomy.
- Remove pan-African language and foreign-country profile options from member-facing discovery.
- Audit existing content and profile defaults for geographic inconsistency.

### 2. Reliable social core
- Test registration, profiles, feed, follows, comments, reactions, messaging, Moments and notifications.
- Verify database Row Level Security and storage permissions for every member journey.
- Complete reporting, blocking and moderation tools before broad public growth.

### 3. Discover Kenya
- Add county and town filters to creative discovery.
- Highlight local circles, gatherings, challenges and collaboration calls.
- Support Kenyan language and discipline filters.
- Add transparent, abuse-resistant trending signals.

### 4. Creator media
- Improve the Moments viewer and media library.
- Build a vertical short-video experience only after upload, moderation, bandwidth and storage controls are reliable.
- Add captions and accessible playback.

### 5. Creator economy
- Improve portfolio presentation, marketplace enquiries and listing trust.
- Add payments only with clear fees, receipts, refunds, disputes, fraud controls and a tested Kenyan payment provider integration.

### 6. Growth and sustainability
- Make the web app installable as a PWA.
- Optimise for affordable Android devices and constrained data connections.
- Measure weekly active members, 4-week retention, meaningful interactions, county representation, completed collaborations and moderation response times.

## What not to claim yet
Do not describe Afrofemme as launch-ready until migrations, storage policies, RLS, build checks and critical user journeys have been tested. Do not claim a finished Reels recommendation system or in-app payments until those capabilities are implemented and verified.
